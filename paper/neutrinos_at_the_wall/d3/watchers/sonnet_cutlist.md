8. **"Turn vs run" for the neutrino step is not resolved by the data, but the text reports it as measured.** l.127 and caption: "turn of 23.4° ... held" vs charged steps run. g = (z-1)²/(4z): z = 5.755 gives a 43.3° turn; z = 5.807, 23.4°; z = 5.859 (+1σ) gives a RUN of length 0.49. Give the angle with its interval, or say the data cannot discriminate turn from run. The "charged all run" half is solid (μ→τ run 7.95).
