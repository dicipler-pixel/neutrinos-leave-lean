\subsection*{A.13 McCormick's self-scattering term is the flux--shear link}
On $N_\mu$ symmetric midpoint bins with weights $w_k=\Delta\mu/2$ and inner product $\langle f,g\rangle=\sum_kw_kf_kg_k$, let $p_0=1$, $p_1=\mu/\lVert\mu\rVert$, and complete to an orthonormal basis $p_0,\dots,p_{N_\mu-1}$ by Gram--Schmidt on $1,\mu,\mu^2,\dots$. Write $P_0f=\langle p_0,f\rangle p_0$ and $P_1f=\langle p_1,f\rangle p_1$. For one frozen group, $\varepsilon/4\pi$ is the bin mean of $I$, so $\varepsilon/4\pi-I=-(I-P_0)I$. The dipole coefficient of the common-frame target is $c=q/(2\pi\sum_k\mu_k^2\Delta\mu)$, chosen so that $c\mu$ carries the group's flux; on the bins $c\mu=P_1I$. Hence the collision term is
\[C I=-\kappa_e(I-P_0)I-\kappa_n(I-P_0-P_1)I .\]
Since $\langle1,\mu\rangle=0$ on symmetric bins, $P_0$ and $P_1$ are orthogonal projections onto orthogonal lines, and in the basis $\{p_\ell\}$, $C=-\operatorname{diag}(0,\kappa_e,\kappa_e+\kappa_n,\dots,\kappa_e+\kappa_n)$. This is the damping matrix of Appendix~A.1 with $\kappa_1=\kappa_e$, $\kappa_2=\kappa_e+\kappa_n$, so by the split-rate identity there
\[\tfrac18\lVert[L,L^\ast]\rVert_F^2=k^2\bigl(c_0^2\kappa_e^2+c_1^2\kappa_n^2\bigr),\]
which in the continuum ($c_0^2=\tfrac13$, $c_1^2=\tfrac4{15}$) is $k^2(\kappa_e^2/3+4\kappa_n^2/15)$. On the bins the streaming couplings are $c_\ell=\langle p_\ell,\mu p_{\ell+1}\rangle$; the values $c_0^2=\tfrac13-\Delta\mu^2/12$ and $c_1^2=\tfrac4{15}(1-\Delta\mu^2)$ hold to machine precision at $N_\mu=32,64,128,1024$ and are not proved here. The momentum exchanged with the plasma is $\kappa_eq$ only: the self-scattering term has $\langle p_1,(I-P_0-P_1)I\rangle=0$. $\square$

\subsection*{A.14 The hidden sector of the chain is fully observable}
Let $B=-ikM-D$ with $M$ the tridiagonal streaming matrix of Section~\ref{sec:cut} (couplings $c_\ell\neq0$) and $D$ diagonal; let $R$ project onto $\ell\le2$ and $Q$ embed $\ell\ge3$. The observability matrix of the pair $(R,B)$ has rows $RB^j$. Because $B$ is tridiagonal with nonzero off-diagonal entries $-ikc_\ell$, the entry $(RB^j)_{2,2+j}$ collects only the path that climbs one rung per step from $\ell=2$ to $\ell=2+j$, so $(RB^j)_{2,2+j}=(-ik)^jc_2c_3\cdots c_{j+1}\neq0$ for $k\neq0$, whatever $D$ is. The rows $RB^j$, $j=0,\dots,L-2$, therefore reach every moment, the pair is observable, and $N_\infty=\{0\}$ for the frozen-time operator. For a smooth time-dependent $D(\tau)$ the Gramian $\mathcal G_T$ is positive definite for every $T>\tau_s$. Suppose $h^\ast\mathcal G_Th=0$; then $y(\tau)=RU(\tau,\tau_s)Qh$ vanishes on $[\tau_s,T]$, so all its derivatives vanish at $\tau_s$. Let $\ell_0\ge3$ be the lowest moment with $h_{\ell_0}\ne0$. Each derivative of $U$ is a sum of products of $B$ and of derivatives of $D$; the diagonal factors cannot change $\ell$, and only the streaming part climbs one rung per factor. So the $\ell=2$ component of the $(\ell_0-2)$-th derivative of $y$ at $\tau_s$ is $(-ik)^{\ell_0-2}c_2\cdots c_{\ell_0-1}h_{\ell_0}\ne0$, a contradiction. Its eigenvalues then rank the hidden directions by the integrated squared readout they produce; $h^\ast\mathcal G_Th=\int_{\tau_s}^T\lVert RU(\tau,\tau_s)Qh\rVert^2d\tau$ follows from the definition. Averaging over $\pm k$ commutes with the parity $\ell\mapsto(-1)^\ell$, so the averaged Gramian's eigenvectors can be chosen of pure parity. $\square$

\subsection*{A.15 Matched states at a restart surface}
Let $p_\ell$ ($\ell\ge3$) be orthogonal on the 32 bins to $1$, $\mu$ and $\mu^2$ under the bin weights. Then $\delta I_{ik}=f\,b_i\,m_i\,(\varepsilon_i/4\pi)\,p_\ell(\mu_k)$ has zero discrete moments of orders $0$, $1$ and $2$ in every cell, so the neutrino energy density, flux and radial pressure of the two states $I\pm\delta I$ are equal cell by cell. The stress--energy tensor of the neutrinos in the spherical solver is built from exactly these three moments, so the sources of the Hamiltonian and momentum constraints, the lapse equation and the fluid equations are equal; with the fluid and metric arrays copied unchanged, every field of the restart state except the hidden part of $I$ is identical, and the constraint residual is identical. Positivity of $I\pm\delta I$ in every bin holds if $|f|\,b_i\,m_i\,|p_\ell(\mu_k)|<4\pi I_{ik}/\varepsilon_i$ for all $k$; the margin $m_i=\min_k[4\pi I_{ik}/\varepsilon_i]/\max_k|p_\ell(\mu_k)|$ makes $|f|<1$ sufficient, and $f=\pm\tfrac12$ uses half of it. On an isotropic cell the bound is $|a|<1/\max_k|p_3(\mu_k)|=1.21498$ for $\ell=3$, and $+2.3509$, $-1.3885$ for $\ell=4$. $\square$
