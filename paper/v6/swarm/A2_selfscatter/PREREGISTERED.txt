READING 1 (the frozen link law, primary)
McCormick's C3 with the first-order target is the v5.1 all-l>=2 family exactly: flux at k1 = k_e,s, every l>=2 at
k2 = k_e,s + k_n,s, so r_s = (k_e,s + k_n,s)/k_e,s >= 1, with k_e,s IDENTICAL in his two runs (nunu only zeroes k_n).
That is the v5.1 intervention (flux rate held, shear+higher rate changed), with r now depending on group, flavour and time.
- r_s(t): from run_mg.rates(Tb) exactly as the code evaluates it on the background (FD rates, chi_e at the background
  temperature, both rates capped at 100 H, 6 groups 0.4..20, two flavours), in the transport phase (after the switch
  from the perfect fluid at total nu_mu,tau rate at x=1 = 100 H).
- Weighting (fixed now): the link rapidity u_s(t) = (1/2) ln r_s(t) is averaged
  (a) over time with the VISIBILITY of the flux link of that group, g_s = (k_e,s/H) exp(-tau_e,s) per d ln a,
      tau_e,s = integral of k_e,s dt to the future. Why: in v5.1 the law was measured with the flux rate's decoupling
      history held fixed and r constant; the flux link is what lets the neutrinos go, so the rapidity it reads is the
      one in force while the flux link releases — the last-scattering density of that link.
  (b) over groups and flavours with the background energy weights (flavour 1/3, 2/3 times the Fermi-Dirac energy
      fraction of each group on the 0.4..20 grid, as run_mg.spectral_weights at tau = 1). Why: the threshold reads the
      neutrino anisotropic stress, which is energy-weighted.
  The rapidity is averaged (not r), because the v5.1 law is linear in the rapidity.
- Prediction: tau_G(with) = tau_G(without) + rate * u_eff, rate = 1.11 (all-l>=2 family; primary) and 1.19-1.20
  (shear-only bracket), with tau_G(without) from McCormick's measured 0.52639 and the references above. Converted back
  to delta_c(with) and to a predicted shift in G.
- Sensitivity weightings, reported but NOT deciding: visibility of the total rate k_e+k_n; uniform in ln a while
  0.1 < k_e,s/H < 10; v5.1's memory-form readout epochs eps* = 1.7 and 4.0 mixed 0.89/0.11.

READING 2 (the opposite: timing, not link rapidity)
Self-scattering keeps the neutrinos isotropic longer: it delays decoupling, as if it were part of the flux rate.
Then the threshold follows a single-rate decoupling temperature set by the TOTAL rate, and the link rapidity plays no role.
- Tdec_eff is where the energy-weighted mean rate over groups and flavours (same weights as above) equals H on the
  background, computed from the same rate functions: from k_e alone (no nu-nu) and from k_e + k_n (with nu-nu).
- Each Tdec_eff is mapped to a threshold by McCormick's single-rate family at T_H = 1 MeV (listed above), linear in
  ln Tdec. Predicts delta_c(with), delta_c(without) and their shift in G.
- Same kill line applied to Reading 2's predicted shift in G (and its two absolute thresholds reported).
Both readings predict the same direction (nu-nu lowers delta_c); the size separates them, and if both pass, the
numbers do not separate them and the decisive simulation is stated instead.
