\section{The author's question, and the setting of the measurements}\label{sec:frame}

\paragraph{The question, in the author's words.} ``not sure there is a first moment my point is i see a way beyond big bang'' (the author, 14 November 2026, verbatim). The picture behind it, in the same notes: ``a self recurring transport system, a closed but growing system that eventually grows to a full relaxation then starts again; light leaves the lightest trail, all else follows and carves.'' This paper asks what the neutrino state must keep because that question needs an answer to it: if the universe relaxes and lets go again, what record crosses the letting-go, and does the black-hole threshold read it?

\paragraph{The author's frame.} The frame is the author's own: Dirac Time~\cite{DT}, in which time is retained history and the record comes before the clock, and the intrinsic geometry of kept subspaces (IQGT~\cite{IQGT}), in which a kept sector is read through the projector of a cluster held by a gap, not through a single eigenvector. The black-hole work \emph{Beyond the Event Horizon}~\cite{BH} supplies the wall, the fold, one-way transport and the orientation record (Section~\ref{sec:bhreadings}). Two statements of that frame are machine-checked and bear directly on this paper. In Dirac Time, two states agree on every future observation exactly when they agree on the first $\dim V$ of them, and the present reading needs no memory exactly when $RL=AR$ for some linear $A$ (\texttt{future\_decided\_by\_first\_dim\_steps}, \texttt{present\_observation\_closes\_iff}; repository \texttt{dicipler-pixel/dirac-time-lean}, commit \texttt{e8a4c86}); the readout Gramian of Section~\ref{sec:gramian} is that predictive quotient for the neutrino chain, and the failure of every instantaneous closure on density, flux and stress is the case $RL\ne AR$. In VOTKP, a reduced record cannot fix the future: two hidden states with the same reduced record transmit $0$ and $\tfrac45$ at a later point (\texttt{ReducedRecord}, Sec.~5.9; \texttt{dicipler-pixel/votkp-lean}, commit \texttt{fa9f8ea})~\cite{VOTKP}; the door of Section~\ref{sec:door} is the same statement measured in a collapse \grade{reading}. Where this paper connects a measured number to that frame, the connection is marked \grade{reading}: an interpretation, not isolated by a run.

\paragraph{The setting the numbers were measured in.} Every number marked \grade{measured} is a threshold search in S.~McCormick's public code~\cite{McCcode} (a preprint, not peer reviewed~\cite{McC}): spherical symmetry, a radiation background, a single relaxation-time collision term (six groups in Section~\ref{sec:mcc}), $R_\nu=0.409$, and runs that start at $\epsilon_0=0.1$ with the hidden moments empty. That empty start is a property of the setting, the standard radiation-era start. It is not a claim of this paper, and it is the one thing the author's question puts in doubt. McCormick's model is the laboratory here, not the frame.

\paragraph{Others who go beyond a first moment.} Penrose's conformal cyclic cosmology carries information across aeons through conformal geometry~\cite{CCC}; Steinhardt and Turok's cyclic model does it through colliding branes~\cite{ST}; Boyle, Finn and Turok read the universe after the bang as the CPT image of the one before it~\cite{BFT}. The reading here differs in its carrier: a kept transport sector and a hidden angular record, with a measured channel (the door) by which a record at decoupling reaches matter.

\paragraph{The author's position, and the conformal boundary.} The draft d4 of the companion paper~\cite{WallD4} states the position this frame writes in: the author does not hold that the universe began in a Big Bang, and there may be no first moment. His framework ``begins from memory, not from a metric, a clock, a particle ontology, or an assumed time coordinate''~\cite{DT}. That draft reads the apparent beginning as a passage between aeons in the sense of Penrose's conformal cyclic cosmology~\cite{CCC}; the comparison with Penrose was relayed on the author's behalf and is not a quotation of his \grade{author's interpretation}. Inside one aeon the flat radiation solution is already conformal to a regular metric, $a^2(-d\eta^2+d\mathbf x^2)=\check\Omega^2 g$ with $\check\Omega=a_1\eta$ and $g=-d\eta^2+d\mathbf x^2$, so the degeneracy at $\eta=0$ is the zero of the conformal factor and $g$ continues across it. The draft adds that Penrose's reciprocal hypothesis makes an empty previous aeon de Sitter space with $\Lambda=3a_1^2$ (checked symbolically there, not rechecked here). In the author's words, ``the wall is not a memory it the force of one via all teh dirac time work''. Read at the conformal boundary, the metric and the clock fail there but the record need not, which has the logical shape of Dirac Time's ``failure of one metric description $\not\Rightarrow$ failure of every spectral record''~\cite{DT}. That is an analogy, not an instance: the projector metric is not the spacetime metric, and no map from the neutrino kept sector to a projector family across the boundary is given \grade{reading}. Neutrinos are massive and a mass scale breaks conformal invariance, so what the neutrino mass scale does at the boundary is left open here, as in~\cite{WallD4}.
